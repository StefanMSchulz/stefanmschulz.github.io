About

I'm a second-year PhD candidate at the University of Bonn working on Differentiable Scene Representations for 4D/6D Capture, Reconstruction and Synthesis.

My work aims to create scalable, high-fidelity, real-time algorithms for acquiring and generating real-world 3D content, with applications in robotics and AR/VR. My current focus is compression and streaming of 4D Gaussian Splatting. If you're interested in this direction, feel free to send me a note.

Outside of work you'll find me on hiking trails with a camera (mostly landscape photography), practicing on my Hummingbird Studio guitar, or reading a sci-fi novel.

    Deep Interpolation of Remote Sensing Land Surface Temperature Data with Partial Convolutions

    Florian Huber*, Stefan Schulz*, Volker Steinhage
